What was wrong
When someone texted you, your phone spoke up twice. The first time was too early — the message hadn't finished arriving yet, so it announced an empty envelope with no sender and no words in it. The real message showed up a second or two later.

The problem: both halves looked like the same message, so once the empty one was counted, the real one got waved through as "already seen" — and quietly vanished. That's how a real time-sensitive invitation nearly got dropped.

Now: the phone waits for the actual message before saying anything.

3-Lane Cards — shipped & proven

Presenter cards now carry three lanes on your "does this invite you?" axis. Status is hard-required — a card with no status is rejected, no more silent "fyi." The docket renders off the submitted status + recap, not the card title.

2. The hard-required error actually fires

Captured live from the real present_to_user code path. Each of these is a card that got REJECTED before it could reach you:

Card sent with NO status
status is required — pick one lane: blocked | weigh_in | fyi. (blocked = I can't proceed without you; weigh_in = I'm moving but you'd want to steer; fyi = nothing to do.)
Card sent with NO recap
recap is required — a short line matching your lane: blocked = what you need from Josh; weigh_in = what to weigh in on; fyi = a short recap of what this is + where it stands.
Card sent with a bogus status
status must be one of blocked | weigh_in | fyi — got "urgent_pls".

The old behavior — silently defaulting a statusless card to "fyi" — is gone. A sender must pick a lane, every time.

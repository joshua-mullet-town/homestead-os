The strip at the top — proposed

BUSY · 21 sessions
One word for how hard it's working, and the number that means something to you.
Why a word, not a percent: the percent was never the useful part — you only ever want to know is my machine OK right now. So it says that directly: EASY · BUSY · SLAMMED.

The three fixes inside that

✕Load 6.96 · 6.03 · 4.86
✓7 of 8 engines going
  • Your machine has 8 engines. That line was always a count of how many are in use — it just never said so. Three numbers became one, in English.
✕RAM 15.8GB / 16.0GB
✓Tight — overflowing to disk
  • "Almost 16 of 16" looks like a five-alarm fire but is normal — the machine always fills unused memory with scratch it can throw away instantly. The honest alarm is different, and right now it's real: you're 8.4GB deep into overflow-to-disk. That's the thing that actually makes it feel slow, and today it's genuinely bad — so the new version says so plainly instead of crying wolf every single day.
✕Claude 21 / MCP 140 / Orphans / Total
✓Sessions / Helpers / Left behind
  • Same numbers, words you'd actually say. "Total" is dropped — it was just the other rows added up.
